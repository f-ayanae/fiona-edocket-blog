      Third-Party AI/Model Review

      Can a bank adopt a credit bureau's new score before Germany's scoring rules change on 20 November 2026?

      November 2026PythonPublic evidence only
      Photo: Michielverbeek, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
      The problem

      From 20 November 2026, German law bans address data, age and other categories from credit scores and gives lenders new duties when they decline. Lenders rely on bureau scores they did not build, yet remain responsible for them.

      What I built

      A Python pipeline: an evidence register of 49 sources, a tested points-table calculator, coded decision rules and an outcome test kit proven on 303,949 loans.

      The result

      Not yet approvable, with a clear path to approval. Two pieces of evidence decide it, and the tests to run on them are already built.
